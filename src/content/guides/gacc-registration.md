## Overview

All egg product manufacturers in China that wish to export must be registered with the **General Administration of Customs of China (GACC)**. This registration is a mandatory prerequisite before any egg product can leave Chinese customs for international markets.

The registration process ensures that exporting facilities meet both Chinese domestic standards and the importing country's food safety requirements.

## Regulatory Framework

### Key Standards

| Standard | Title | Scope |
|----------|-------|-------|
| GB 2749-2015 | National Food Safety Standard — Eggs | Fresh egg safety requirements |
| GB 21710-2016 | National Food Safety Standard — Egg Products | Processed egg product safety |
| SN/T 1.29 | Export Egg Product Inspection Regulations | Export inspection procedures |

### Governing Bodies

- **GACC** — Overall export registration and customs clearance
- **Local Customs (formerly CIQ)** — On-site inspection, health certificate issuance
- **SAMR** — Domestic food production license (prerequisite)

## 6-Step Export Registration Process

### Step 1: Obtain Domestic Food Production License

Before applying for export registration, your facility must hold a valid **Food Production License (食品生产许可证)** issued by the local branch of the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR).

**Requirements:**
- Facility meets GB 14881 (General Hygienic Practice for Food Production)
- HACCP or equivalent food safety management system implemented
- Qualified laboratory or third-party testing arrangement

### Step 2: Apply for GACC Export Food Registration

Submit your application through the **GACC Internet+ Customs** online platform (互联网+海关).

**Required documents:**
- Food Production License (copy)
- Business License (营业执照)
- Facility layout drawings
- HACCP plan documentation
- Product list with HS codes
- Quality management system documentation

### Step 3: Facility Inspection

Local customs authorities will schedule an on-site inspection covering:

- **Production environment** — Cleanliness, pest control, waste management
- **Processing equipment** — Condition, calibration, maintenance records
- **Laboratory capabilities** — Testing equipment, method validation
- **Traceability system** — Raw material sourcing to finished product tracking
- **Staff qualifications** — Training records, health certificates

### Step 4: Corrective Actions (if needed)

If non-conformities are found during inspection:

1. Receive detailed non-conformity report
2. Develop corrective action plan with timelines
3. Implement corrections
4. Request re-inspection
5. Obtain clearance

### Step 5: Registration Approval

Upon successful inspection:

- Facility receives a **GACC export registration number**
- Registration is valid for **4 years** (renewable)
- Facility is added to the GACC registered establishment list
- Some destination countries require the facility to also be on their approved list

### Step 6: Maintain Compliance

Ongoing requirements after registration:

- **Annual self-inspection** — Document and report results
- **Regulatory updates** — Monitor changes in destination country requirements
- **Record keeping** — Maintain production, testing, and shipping records for minimum 2 years
- **Report changes** — Notify GACC of any significant facility or process changes

## Residue Compliance Warning

Egg products are subject to strict **veterinary drug residue** monitoring. Key substances monitored include:

| Category | Examples | Maximum Residue Limits |
|----------|----------|----------------------|
| Antibiotics | Fluoroquinolones, Tetracyclines | Per GB 31650-2019 |
| Anti-parasitic drugs | Fipronil, Nicarbazin | Zero tolerance / strict limits |
| Hormones | Estradiol, Progesterone | Not permitted in laying hens |
| Heavy metals | Lead, Cadmium, Mercury | Per GB 2762-2022 |

**Critical**: Fipronil contamination has caused major international recalls. Ensure your egg supply chain has **zero exposure** to fipronil-containing pesticides used in poultry housing.

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Incomplete HACCP documentation** — Ensure your HACCP plan specifically addresses egg product hazards
2. **Traceability gaps** — Must trace from farm/supplier through to export shipment
3. **Outdated testing methods** — Ensure lab methods align with current national standards
4. **Ignoring destination country requirements** — GACC registration alone may not satisfy importing country requirements
