## Overview

The **health certificate (卫生证书)** is a mandatory export document issued by Chinese customs authorities certifying that a shipment of egg products meets food safety requirements. Without a valid health certificate, egg products cannot clear Chinese customs for export or be accepted at the destination country's border.

## Who Issues It

The health certificate is issued by the **local customs office** (formerly the CIQ — China Inspection and Quarantine authority) in the jurisdiction where the exporting facility is located.

## Prerequisites

Before applying for a health certificate, your facility must have:

1. **GACC export registration** — Active and current (see [GACC Registration Guide](/en/guides/gacc-registration))
2. **Domestic food production license** — Valid license from local SAMR branch
3. **Batch production records** — Complete traceability documentation for the export batch
4. **Laboratory testing results** — From an accredited laboratory covering required parameters

## Application Process

### Step 1: Prepare Documentation Package

Assemble the following for each export batch:

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Export contract or purchase order | Confirms commercial basis for export |
| Product specification sheet | Describes product type, packaging, storage requirements |
| Batch production records | Demonstrates traceability from raw material to finished product |
| Laboratory test report | Covers microbiological, chemical residue, and physical parameters |
| Packaging and labeling samples | Shows compliance with destination country requirements |

### Step 2: Submit Application

- Submit through the **China Single Window (单一窗口)** online platform
- Select the appropriate commodity code and destination country
- Upload all required documentation
- Pay applicable inspection and certification fees

### Step 3: Customs Inspection

The local customs office will:

1. **Review documentation** for completeness and compliance
2. **Conduct or verify laboratory testing** — may require additional sampling
3. **Inspect the production facility** if due for scheduled review
4. **Verify packaging and labeling** against destination country requirements

### Step 4: Certificate Issuance

Upon successful inspection:

- Health certificate is issued in the **format required by the destination country**
- Some destinations require specific certificate templates — confirm with customs
- Certificate typically valid for a **limited period** — ship promptly after issuance
- Digital and physical copies may be needed depending on destination

## Destination-Specific Requirements

Different destination countries may require specific health certificate content:

| Destination | Special Requirements |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Hong Kong | Standard GACC format accepted |
| Singapore | SFA-compatible format, specific testing parameters |
| Japan | MHLW-specified format, additional quarantine declarations |
| Malaysia | Halal-related declarations may be required on certificate |

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Issue: Certificate Rejected at Destination

**Causes:**
- Wrong certificate format for destination country
- Missing required declarations or testing parameters
- Certificate expired before shipment arrival

**Prevention:**
- Confirm required format with destination importer before application
- Build time buffers between certificate issuance and shipment
- Verify all destination-specific requirements are included

### Issue: Testing Delays

**Causes:**
- Laboratory backlog during peak export seasons
- Re-testing required due to non-conforming results

**Prevention:**
- Maintain ongoing testing program rather than batch-only testing
- Use accredited laboratories with reliable turnaround times
- Address any production quality issues before testing

## Key Reminders

- **One certificate per shipment** — each export batch needs its own health certificate
- **Certificate must match actual shipment** — product type, quantity, and destination must match
- **Retain copies** for minimum 2 years as required by GACC regulations
- **Plan ahead** — application to issuance can take 5-15 business days depending on testing
