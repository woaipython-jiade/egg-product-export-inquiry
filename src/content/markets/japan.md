## Market Access Overview

Japan is accessible for certain Chinese egg products, but under a **highly regulated and condition-sensitive** framework. Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) jointly enforce some of the world's strictest food safety and animal quarantine standards.

Access is restricted rather than closed — meaning trade is possible but contingent on meeting strict requirements and subject to disruption from disease outbreak events.

### Key Characteristics

- **Strict food safety regime** — Japan's "positive list" system for agricultural chemicals and veterinary drugs
- **HPAI-sensitive** — Avian influenza outbreaks trigger rapid region-based import suspensions
- **High-value market** — Premium pricing for products meeting Japanese quality standards
- **Detailed documentation** — Extensive paperwork and compliance verification required

## Required Documents

1. **GACC Registration** — Facility must be registered and approved for Japan-destined exports
2. **Health Certificate** — Specific format required by Japan, issued by Chinese authorities
3. **Animal Quarantine Certificate** — Issued by MAFF-recognized Chinese quarantine authorities
4. **Product Testing Report** — Must cover Japan's positive list parameters
5. **Certificate of Origin**
6. **Import Notification** — Filed by Japanese importer with MHLW quarantine station

## Regulatory Authorities

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| MHLW (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare) | Food safety, import inspection, positive list |
| MAFF (Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries) | Animal quarantine, HPAI controls |
| GACC | Chinese export registration and health certification |

## Standards & Testing

- **Positive list system**: Japan's comprehensive system sets maximum residue limits (MRLs) for all agricultural chemicals — substances not on the list default to a uniform limit of 0.01 ppm
- **Microbiological standards**: Strict limits per Japan's Food Sanitation Act
- **Heavy metals**: Lead, cadmium, mercury limits per Japanese standards
- **Aflatoxin**: Total aflatoxin limit of 10 μg/kg
- **Labeling**: Japanese-language labeling required under JAS (Japanese Agricultural Standards) system

## Risk Notes

- **HPAI outbreaks are the primary trade disruptor** — Japan suspends imports from affected Chinese provinces rapidly; suspensions can persist for months after outbreak clearance
- **Positive list violations** are treated very seriously — even trace amounts of non-listed substances can result in shipment rejection and enhanced scrutiny on future shipments
- **Enrofloxacin and fluoroquinolone residues** are particularly sensitive given historical incidents
- **Import inspection rate** may be elevated for Chinese egg products due to compliance history
- Japan requires **pre-notification** through the MHLW quarantine system before shipment arrives
