## Market Access Overview

Hong Kong and Macau are among the most accessible export destinations for Chinese egg products. Both Special Administrative Regions (SARs) maintain open import policies for egg products originating from mainland China, subject to standard food safety and documentation requirements.

### Key Advantages

- **No quota restrictions** on egg product imports from China
- **Geographic proximity** reduces logistics costs and transit times
- **Established trade channels** with well-known import procedures
- **Shared cultural preferences** for egg product varieties

## Required Documents

1. **Health Certificate** — Issued by local CIQ (China Inspection and Quarantine) authorities
2. **Certificate of Origin** — Confirming mainland China production
3. **Product Testing Report** — Meeting Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety (CFS) standards
4. **GACC Registration** — Exporting facility must be registered with General Administration of Customs of China
5. **Import License** — Obtained by the Hong Kong/Macau importer

## Regulatory Authorities

| Authority | Jurisdiction | Role |
|-----------|-------------|------|
| Hong Kong Centre for Food Safety (CFS) | Hong Kong | Food import regulation and testing |
| Municipal Affairs Bureau (IAM) | Macau | Food safety regulation |
| GACC | China | Export facility registration and inspection |

## Standards & Testing

- **Microbiological standards**: Salmonella, E. coli, total plate count
- **Chemical residue limits**: Antibiotics, heavy metals, pesticide residues
- **Labeling requirements**: Product name, ingredients, production date, expiry date, storage conditions (bilingual EN/ZH for HK)

## Risk Notes

- Hong Kong CFS conducts **routine surveillance testing** at import points — ensure all products meet published standards
- **Melamine testing** remains a standard check for egg products from China (legacy of 2008 incident)
- Macau may have **additional labeling requirements** in Portuguese

## Action Checklist

- [ ] Verify GACC registration is current for your facility
- [ ] Obtain health certificate from local CIQ
- [ ] Prepare bilingual product labels meeting CFS requirements
- [ ] Engage a registered importer in HK/Macau
- [ ] Arrange pre-shipment testing at an accredited laboratory
- [ ] Ensure cold chain logistics if shipping temperature-sensitive products
