## Market Access Overview

Singapore is one of Southeast Asia's most transparent and well-regulated food import markets. The Singapore Food Agency (SFA) oversees all food imports with clear, published requirements. China is an approved source country for egg products, making this a viable export destination for compliant manufacturers.

### Key Characteristics

- **Transparent regulatory framework** — SFA publishes clear import requirements and approved source lists
- **High food safety standards** — Rigorous testing and inspection regime at import
- **Premium market positioning** — Higher price points for quality products
- **English-language documentation** — Reduces translation barriers for regulatory compliance

## Required Documents

1. **GACC Registration** — Exporting facility must be registered with China's General Administration of Customs
2. **Health Certificate** — Issued by Chinese customs authorities (CIQ)
3. **SFA Import Permit** — Obtained by the Singapore importer through the SFA online system
4. **Product Testing Report** — Covering microbiological and chemical residue parameters
5. **Certificate of Origin** — Confirming Chinese origin

## Regulatory Authority

| Authority | Role |
|-----------|------|
| Singapore Food Agency (SFA) | Food import regulation, testing, approval |
| GACC | Chinese export registration and inspection |

## Standards & Testing

- **Microbiological limits**: Salmonella (absent in 25g), E. coli, total plate count per SFA Food Regulations
- **Chemical residue testing**: Veterinary drug residues, heavy metals, pesticides
- **Labeling**: English-language labeling required — product name, ingredients, allergen declaration, country of origin, expiry date
- **Packaging**: Must meet SFA food contact material requirements

## Risk Notes

- SFA conducts **random sampling and testing** at the point of import — non-compliant shipments will be rejected or destroyed
- Singapore has **zero tolerance for certain residues** — ensure complete veterinary drug withdrawal compliance
- **Aflatoxin limits** are strictly enforced for egg products
- Import permit applications require **advance processing time** — plan logistics accordingly
